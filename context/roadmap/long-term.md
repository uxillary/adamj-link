# Long Term

## Vision

adamj.link should become the central hub for everything I build.

The website should evolve alongside my projects and become a living archive of my work, ideas, experiments and progress.

---

## Long-Term Goals

- Fully dynamic project ecosystem.
- Live dashboards powered by real data.
- Rich project timelines.
- Interactive research logs.
- Public development journal.
- Automated GitHub integrations.
- Cross-project analytics.
- Unified design language across every project.
- AI-assisted content generation where appropriate.
- A memorable personal brand that stands apart from traditional portfolios.
