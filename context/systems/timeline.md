# Timeline

## Purpose

The timeline communicates what I am currently focused on.

Unlike a traditional portfolio, it should show progression rather than simply listing projects.

---

## Design Goals

The timeline should feel:

- alive
- chronological
- informative
- interactive

---

## Behaviour

The current point should update automatically when appropriate.

Previous milestones remain visible.

Future milestones communicate direction.

---

## Principles

The timeline should explain the journey rather than simply list dates.
