# vision.md — adamj.link

## The “Why”

adamj.link is becoming a central hub for Adam Johnston’s online work, projects, creative identity, and technical presence.

It is intentionally designed as something beyond a traditional portfolio.

The site acts as:

- a living portfolio
- a creative technical identity
- a dynamic showcase of activity
- a gateway into projects and experiments
- a personal internet ecosystem

The experience should feel alive, evolving, intentional, and personal.

Rather than simply displaying finished work, the site should demonstrate:

- technical ability
- creativity
- obsessive attention to detail
- experimentation
- consistency
- momentum
- personality
- real-world activity

The site should communicate:
> “This developer is creative, knows what he is doing, and genuinely builds things.”

---

## Emotional Direction

The overall emotional direction should feel:

- cool
- technical
- modern
- internet-native
- visually satisfying
- polished
- slightly mysterious
- experimental
- ahead of the curve

The experience should feel like:
> “the future is now”

without becoming overwhelming, corporate, or gimmicky.

---

## Intended Audience

The site is primarily aimed toward:

- potential collaborators
- creative partners
- potential clients
- followers and subscribers
- developers
- creators
- technically curious people
- future employers or opportunities

The experience should feel approachable to both technical and non-technical users through strong UX and presentation.

---

## Core Philosophy

The philosophy behind adamj.link is:

- blend personality with competence
- show real activity instead of polished fakery
- create something memorable instead of generic
- prioritise intentionality over trend-chasing
- feel handcrafted rather than mass-generated
- combine technical depth with accessibility
- present expert knowledge in a visually approachable way

The site should feel:

- organised
- thoughtful
- cohesive
- alive
- trustworthy

The project should feel like:
> the opposite of AI slop.

---

## What Makes It Different

Unlike many portfolios, adamj.link should not feel:

- corporate
- templated
- bootstrap-looking
- generic Tailwind SaaS
- startup-like
- overdesigned
- fake
- inactive
- childish
- AI-generated

Instead, the site should feel:

- dynamic
- intentional
- custom-built
- evolving
- technically impressive
- personal
- memorable

The experience should reward exploration through:

- live statistics
- active project updates
- subtle interactions
- polished UX
- evolving systems
- real development activity

The goal is for visitors to leave curious and wanting to know more.

---

## Design & UX Direction

The visual direction should balance:

- polish
- uniqueness
- calmness
- experimentation
- readability
- subtle futuristic undertones

The site should avoid excessive visual noise or trend-heavy design decisions.

Interactions and animations should feel:

- subtle
- satisfying
- lightweight
- responsive
- intentional

Good UX is a core priority.

Technical knowledge should be presented in a way that feels understandable and visually approachable to a broad audience.

---

## Long-Term Vision

Long-term, adamj.link may evolve into:

- a central identity hub
- a launchpad for future projects
- a creative technology ecosystem
- a business platform
- a gateway into larger brands and experiments
- a connected network of projects, tools, media, and communities

The site should continue evolving while maintaining a handcrafted identity and cohesive direction.

The long-term goal is to create:
> a memorable corner of the internet that feels genuinely alive.
