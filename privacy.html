Privacy Policy

This policy explains what data is processed on adamj.link and why.

Owner and contact

This website is operated by Adam Johnston. Questions can be sent to adam@ajstudios.dev or hello@ajstudios.dev.

Analytics

Google Analytics (gtag.js, GA4) is used to measure visits and page usage. This may include page views, approximate location, browser/device information, and engagement events using pseudonymous identifiers.

Cookies and local storage

The site stores preference data in localStorage/sessionStorage to remember theme, corner radius style, and a local view counter cache. Google Analytics may also set cookies for measurement.

Contact form processing

Messages submitted through the contact form are sent to the /api/contact endpoint and forwarded by email so Adam can reply. Form data is used only for handling your enquiry.

Data retention

Contact messages are retained only as long as needed to respond and maintain a practical communication record. Operational logs are kept for short-term reliability and security needs, then removed.

Content classification note

This site contains no adult content.
