Privacy Policy

This page explains what information is collected when you visit this site and how it is used.

Data collection

Server logs may store your IP address, browser details, and pages visited. Logs are used for security and are automatically purged.

Analytics

Basic traffic metrics may be gathered using privacy‑focused analytics. Data is aggregated and never sold.

Contact

If you email or otherwise contact Adam, your details will be used only to respond to your message.

Local storage & cookies

This site uses your browser's localStorage and sessionStorage to remember settings like your theme and to cache basic page-view counts.

No third-party cookies or tracking scripts are used. Because this storage is first-party and required for core features and aggregated analytics, explicit consent is not necessary.

Updates

This policy may change over time. Check this page for the latest version. Questions? Email adam@ajstudios.dev.
